## Description (max 4000 chars)
HOODLINGO is the trivia game that asks what you **really** know.

Not Jeopardy. Not Trivial Pursuit. This is hood trivia — built for the culture, by the culture. Four categories. Real questions. No mercy.

**🎤 RAP** — From Big Daddy Kane to today's hottest. Bars, beefs, and classic albums.
**🏙 THESE STREETS** — Slang, history, neighborhoods, the people who built it.
**🎬 HOOD FLICKS** — Friday, Menace, Boyz n the Hood, New Jack City and beyond.
**🏪 CORNER STORES** — Loosies, chopped cheese, dutches, AriZona. You know.

**HOW IT PLAYS**
• First answer is FINAL — no take-backs
• 2 strikes you're OUT — every question counts
• 10 questions per round, lightning fast
• Earn your spot on the global leaderboard

**OG CONTRIBUTOR PROGRAM**
Top players unlock OG status. Submit your own questions, vote on what makes it in, and shape the next generation of Hoodlingo trivia. Every approved question earns you points and bragging rights.

**CHALLENGE A FRIEND**
Beat a category? Send the score by text. They get one shot to match it. Talk that talk.

**WEEKLY DIGEST**
Climb (or slip) on the leaderboard each Friday. We'll let you know where you stand — and how to get back on top, Jefe.

Welcome to the hood, scholar. Lace up your Timbs.

— *Shant-Knows-It-All Presents*
